# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

NextGenAI is a youth-led community. We are committed to making participation in our project and organization a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, ability, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences, especially from student contributors
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and our mission of ethical AI education

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **contact@nextgenai.org**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
