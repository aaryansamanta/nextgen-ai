# 🌐 Partners & Collaboration Strategy

</div>

NextGenAI builds a robust, multi-stakeholder ecosystem to scale ethical AI education, foster mentorship, secure resources, and drive global impact for youth. Our strategy emphasizes inclusive partnerships that transcend traditional boundaries.

## Academic Partner — [Legend College Preparatory](https://legendcp.com/)

A WASC-accredited private high school in Cupertino, California serving grades 6–12 with a rigorous, UC-approved curriculum. LCP empowers scholars to master Python, data science, machine learning, ethical AI, and emerging fields like AP Cybersecurity. Recognized among the top 20% of private schools in California (WASC, 2023).

## Industry Partner — [Taiwan Artificial Intelligence Industrial Association (TAIA)](https://taiago.com/)

A non-profit accelerating AI industrialization across Taiwan, uniting industry leaders, academic institutions, government agencies, and research entities. Programs include iPAS AI Application Planner certifications, government-subsidized training (AIGO), and the International AI Education Employment Alliance (IAEEA). Strong ties to Hsinchu Science Park, ITRI, Foxconn, and Delta Electronics.

## Non-Profit Partner — [AI Ethos, Inc.](https://www.aiethos.org)

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit transforming global education through ethical, inclusive, and culturally relevant AI tutoring — designing localized, multilingual, community-inclusive programs that prioritize the most underserved learners.

## Key Collaborators

- **High Schools & Universities** — Legend College Preparatory (US & Taiwan) and Southern Taiwan University of Science and Technology (STUST) for AI clubs, curricula, mentorship, and research access.
- **Non-Profits & NGOs** — AI Ethos, Inc. and similar organizations to amplify community programs and bridge access gaps for underserved youth.
- **Industry** — TAIA and partner companies for guest speakers, internships, and real-world projects.
- **Distinguished Faculty** — Advisors from Stanford, Tsinghua, and Nanjing University providing expert guidance at the intersection of AI, healthcare, and ethics.
