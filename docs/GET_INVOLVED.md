# 🙌 Get Involved

**Empower Youth. Shape AI. Act Today.**

</div>

Join the NextGenAI movement and become part of a youth-led revolution in ethical, inclusive AI education. Whether you're a student, educator, school, NGO, or industry partner, your involvement helps build a future where AI benefits everyone — responsibly, equitably, and globally.

## Why Partner with NextGenAI?

- 🚀 **Shape the Future of AI** by empowering the next generation of young leaders to drive responsible innovation.
- 🧑‍💻 **Gain Early Access to Cutting-Edge Talent & Projects** — collaborate with talented high school students creating real-world AI solutions in bias mitigation, medical imaging, physical AI, sustainability, and more.
- 🌍 **Be Part of a Global Movement** for ethical AI education, cross-cultural collaboration, and social good.

## Benefits for Students Who Join

| Benefit | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| AI Knowledge & Skills | Bootcamps, workshops, guest speakers, and UC-approved courses in Python/ML, data science, and ethics |
| Innovation Opportunities | Design and prototype AI-driven solutions, mentored by experts, presented at Showcase Days |
| Global Connections | Network with students, educators, and AI professionals worldwide |
| Leadership Development | Lead initiatives, grow as a Global AI Ambassador |
| Ethical Awareness & Impact | Bias audits, policy workshops, the AI Ethical Awareness Scale |
| Portfolio & Recognition | Projects, presentations, student-led media, standout credentials |
| Community Good | Free literacy workshops for peers and underserved groups |

## Next Steps — How to Get Involved

- **Students & Youth** → Sign up for AI Pathways, join an AI club, or apply to become a Global AI Ambassador.
- **Schools & Educators** → Start or support an AI club with our curriculum, resources, and mentorship.
- **NGOs & Non-Profits** → Collaborate on outreach, funding, workshops, and programs for underserved communities.
- **Industry & Partners** → Provide guest speakers, internships, mentorship, or sponsor real-world projects.
